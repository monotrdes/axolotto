## English

The Jackpot is the beating heart of Axolotto multiplayer. It is a **global, shared prize pool** that grows with every single multiplayer match played across the entire platform. A small 5% slice of every game's total pot is automatically funneled into the Jackpot, making it grow continuously — faster during peak hours, slower during quiet times, but never stopping as long as matches are running.

Winning the Jackpot is the ultimate achievement in Axolotto. To claim it, a player must satisfy ALL of the following conditions simultaneously: complete Premio 1 (their first winning pattern), do so within the **first 4 to 6 cards called** (the brutally hard part), play in a room with at least 5 human boards across at least 2 different wallet addresses (anti-sybil protection), and be a human player — bots cannot trigger it. Statistically, hitting a winning pattern within the first 4 cards of a 54-card deck is roughly a 0.01% chance per game. You need a near-perfect alignment of board coverage, Focus, Salinity, Luck, room composition, and card draws.

When won, **90% of the accumulated Jackpot** is paid to the winner(s). If multiple players hit Premio 1 on the same qualifying turn, the 90% is split equally. The remaining **10% reseeds the next Jackpot**, with a **1,000 FRJ minimum** guaranteed by the system treasury. VIP Axolite holders receive a +5% bonus on their individual share. The Jackpot amount is always visible in the multiplayer lobby, updates in real time as matches complete, and triggers a global notification when someone wins.

Players should treat the Jackpot as a free lottery ticket that comes with every multiplayer game — not as something to chase. The real, consistent income comes from regular Premio 1 and Premio 2 wins, Axolotito staking, and P2P market sales. Play normally, and if the stars ever align for you on that one magical turn... you will become an Axolotto legend.
