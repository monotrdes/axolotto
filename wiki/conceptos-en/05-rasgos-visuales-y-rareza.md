## English

Every Axolotito is visually unique. Its appearance is not random nor purely cosmetic — it is determined by its base statistics (SAL, PILA, OJO, CAR, AGI, SAB, FUE), and each visual trait directly influences the passive FRJ staking rate. The rarer the trait, the higher the FRJ/hour it generates.

There are 7 visual traits: Skin Color (Salinity), Gills (Stamina), Eyes (Focus), Mouth/Expression (Charisma), Tail (Agility), Forehead (Wisdom), and Limbs (Strength). Each trait has 4–5 variants spanning Common (0.01 FRJ/h part bonus) to Mythic/Legendary (0.20 FRJ/h part bonus) tiers.

Skin color determines the base staking multiplier, ranging from 0.05 FRJ/h for Common (Pink, Gray-Light variants) to 0.40 FRJ/h for Epic (Gray-Dead-Sea). The two special skins — Astral (2.50 FRJ/h, 80% from Astral Webito, guarantees at least one premium visual trait) and Gold (1.00 FRJ/h, 20% from Astral Webito) — are the ultimate staking engines.

Total staking rate = Skin Multiplier + sum of all 6 part bonuses, then multiplied by level bonus (1 + 0.1 × level). A full Mythic Axolotito (Gold skin + 6 Mythic parts) generates 2.20 FRJ/h base, and at level 20 this becomes 6.60 FRJ/h. A full Astral with Mythic parts reaches 11.10 FRJ/h at level 20.

Mythic traits (Phoenix Gills, Plasma Tail, Halo Forehead, Divine Mouth, Coral Limbs) have a 25% chance to appear when genetic purity exceeds 110% during imprinting — an extremely rare event. Cosmetic equipment slots (Head, Eyes, Body) are purely aesthetic and obtained via Gashapon, the P2P Market, or special events. Rare visual traits can multiply an Axolotito's market value by 10–50×, making trait hunting one of the core economic and emotional drivers of the Axolotto metaverse.
