# Tips and Strategies — Veteran Wisdom

This is the page every new player should read. It is not theory — it is the collection of things veterans learned through bruises, mistakes, and lost matches. Here is EVERYTHING I wish I knew when I started playing Axolotto.

### Board Building Strategy

Not all cells on your 4x4 Board are equal. The center 4 cells (5, 6, 9, 10) are the most valuable — they participate in horizontal lines, vertical lines, diagonals, cuadritos, pocito, AND cruz patterns. Corners (0, 3, 12, 15) are second most valuable — esquinas plus lines plus diagonals. Edge-mid cells are least valuable. **Place your BEST cards (highest rarity) on center cells, your second-best on corners, and the rest on edges.** For Rookies mode, prioritize line coverage — make sure every row, column, and diagonal has at least 1 strong card. For Champions, also cover cuadritos (center is key) and esquinas (corners are key). Build two Boards: one optimized for lines (Rookies) and one for full coverage (Champions/tournaments). And update your Board regularly — a Board that was good two weeks ago is probably mediocre today.

### Axolotito Build Priorities

**For general play:** Focus > Luck > Stamina. Keep SAL as low as possible — SAL is your enemy. It pushes you to the end of the deck in multiplayer, increases energy costs per match, and raises slip chance. Never raise SAL voluntarily. **For CPU grinding:** Hiperactivo nature (faster sleep = more games per hour). Build for volume over precision. **For Multiplayer:** Metodico (consistent marking, fewer missed cards) or Suertudo (bigger prize shares via Luck bonus). High Focus (80+) and low SAL (<20) are non-negotiable for multiplayer. **Energy management:** feed when below 30 energy, sleep before max energy decays too far. Never let `energy_max_current` drop below 50. Sleep resets max energy to your PILA stat. **Before breeding, level up your padrino first** — the padrino's level floors the egg's stats. Each mentorship awards +150 XP to the padrino, making breeding one of the best leveling methods in the game.

### Economy Tips

Never spend your last 100 FRJ — always keep entry fee + food money. The Banco de Algas (AXF to FRJ converter) is a trap for free-to-play players: AXF is scarce and non-renewable, FRJ is abundant through gameplay. Best value purchases in order: food, entry fees, boards, cave expansion, capsules. VIP Coral (400 AXF/month) pays for itself in FRJ if you play daily through the 1,200 FRJ/month in direct benefits, 15% entry discounts, and bonus rewards. Sell duplicate cards on the P2P market before melting them — a foil epic might fetch 5,000 FRJ on P2P versus 200 FRJ melted.

### Common Beginner Mistakes

1. Playing Champions CPU with a level 1 axo — you will lose 50 FRJ. Stay in Rookies until level 10+.
2. Spending all FRJ on Gashapon capsules — you need FRJ to play, and without playing you cannot earn more.
3. Never sleeping your axo — max energy decays to 10, giving you 1 game per sleep cycle.
4. Melting rare/foil/first-edition cards without checking P2P prices first.
5. Ignoring the daily Lunar Cycle rewards — 530 FRJ/week, completely free.
6. Not staking idle boards and axolotitos — literally free passive income going to waste.
7. Forgetting to claim staking rewards — they cap and excess FRJ is LOST.
8. Chasing the Jackpot — 0.01% chance per game. Play normally and let Jackpot be a pleasant surprise, not a strategy.

### Multiplayer Survival Guide

Start with CPU mode until your axo is level 10+ and your Board has good coverage. Begin in Rookies multiplayer (10 FRJ entry) for low-risk learning. Set stop-loss at 50% of your escrow budget and take-profit at 200% — set them and do NOT touch them. Do not register more Boards than you can afford to lose entry fees on (escrow >= 3x entry_per_board). Watch the lobby for rooms with fewer players for faster starts and less competition. Your SAL stat HURTS in multiplayer — the slip chance and deck position penalties are brutal.

### Breeding for Success

Best padrino: high Focus + high Luck + LOW Salinity. Play CPU Rookies during imprinting for the easiest wins. The padrino gains +150 XP per mentorship — use breeding to level up your best axos. Max 3 mentorships per axolotito, so choose pairings wisely. An Astral Webito guarantees at least Rare visual rarity and a stat floor of 45 across all attributes — it is the single most valuable breeding resource in the game. Do not underestimate your Tutorial Webito — your first axo is perfectly viable and will carry you through early levels.

### Cave Expansion Priority

Level 3 ASAP (unlocks hosting + extra starting card). Then Level 5 (+1 incubation slot, double breeding speed). Then Level 7 (free monthly Foil booster + free Astral Webito — this is where the cave becomes truly profitable). Finally Level 8 (endgame, +10% AXF multiplier). Skip acceleration unless you are rich in AXF — the free eggs from waiting are worth far more than the time saved.

### Gashapon Wisdom

Triple Suerte (22,500 FRJ) is the best value per FRJ if you can afford it without compromising your operational budget. Never roll capsules when below 5,000 FRJ total. The pity/karma system is your friend — every paid roll that does not yield a rare item brings you closer to the next one that WILL. Track your karma mentally. Bronze capsules are great for beginners (cheap, build pity, fun reveals). Gold capsules are for endgame (20,000 FRJ is a serious investment). Free capsules from VIP or the Lunar Cycle do NOT advance pity — only paid rolls count.

### Top 10 Takeaways

1. Best cards go in the center of your Board. Always.
2. Focus > Luck > Stamina. Keep SAL on the floor.
3. Never spend your last 100 FRJ. Always keep entry fee + food.
4. The AXF-to-FRJ bank is an F2P trap.
5. Sleep your axo. Decaying max energy is a disaster.
6. Sell duplicates on P2P before melting. The difference is brutal.
7. Stop-loss at 50%, take-profit at 200%. Set them and do not touch.
8. Cave level 3 is your absolute priority. Hosting + extra card.
9. Never roll capsules below 5,000 FRJ.
10. Have fun. This is a game. If you stress more than you enjoy, slow down.
