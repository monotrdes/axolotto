# Dual Economy — AXF and FRJ

Axolotto uses **two currencies** with very different purposes. Understanding the difference between them is the first thing every new player should know. Here is everything, with no formulas or jargon.

---

## The Two Currencies at a Glance

| Feature | AXF (Axofichas) | FRJ (Frijolitos) |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Premium currency (real money) | Game currency (earned by playing) |
| Is it crypto? | Yes — ERC-20 token on Plasma Chain | No — exists only in the game's database |
| How to get it | Buying with real money | Playing, staking, prizes, VIP, P2P market |
| Can it be withdrawn? | Yes (via DevEx, conditions below) | No (by design — protects the economy) |
| Usable in multiplayer? | **Forbidden** (code-enforced rejection) | **Only currency allowed** |
| Reference value | 1 AXF ≈ $2 MXN (~$0.10 USD) | No real-money value |

## AXF (Axofichas) — The Premium Currency

Axofichas are Axolotto's **on-chain** token. They live on the Plasma blockchain (chain ID 9746) as a real ERC-20 token. This means you actually own your AXF — they are in your wallet, not in a game database.

### How to Get AXF

**Only purchasable with real money.** There is no way to earn AXF by playing — by design. This protects the token's value and complies with regulations.

**Purchase Packages:**

| Package | AXF Received | Price (MXN) | Price per AXF |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 200 AXF | $35 MXN | ~$0.18 MXN each |
| Medium | 550 AXF | $88 MXN | ~$0.16 MXN each |
| Large | 1,725 AXF | $263 MXN | ~$0.15 MXN each |
| Whale | 7,250 AXF | $875 MXN | ~$0.12 MXN each |

**Payment Methods:**
- Credit/debit card (via MoonPay or Mercado Pago)
- SPEI bank transfer
- OXXO cash payment
- USDT or USDC (5-10% discount for using stablecoins)
- ETH or MATIC

### What AXF Buys

- **Webitos (eggs)** — purchase eggs to incubate new Axolotitos
- **Premium Sobrecitos** — booster packs with rare cards and consumables
- **VIP subscription** — Coral, Dorado, or Axolite membership (monthly payment in AXF)
- **Board slot unlocks** — more slots for creating and staking boards
- **Cave expansion acceleration** — skip excavation wait times
- **P2P Market purchases** — buy cards, boards, and Axolotitos from other players

### The 80/20 Reserve Model

For every AXF sold, the system automatically splits:

- **80% to the backup reserve** — untouchable, guarantees solvency for DevEx
- **20% to the operational treasury** — project expenses, development, servers

After taxes and payment gateway fees, the actual net entering the project is approximately **$8.17 MXN** per $10 MXN in AXF sold. This ensures Axolotto is sustainable long-term without relying on new players paying old ones (it is not a Ponzi scheme).

### DevEx — How to Cash Out Real Money

The **DevEx** program (Developer Exchange, inherited from the Roblox ecosystem) lets you convert your in-game progress into real money. Here is the full path:

1. **Play and earn FRJ** through matches, staking, VIP, P2P market
2. **Buy or create valuable assets** — rare cards, high-staking boards, high-stat Axolotitos
3. **Sell those assets on the P2P Market** — receive AXF as payment from other players
4. **Request DevEx** from your user panel — AXF received from P2P sales gets converted to real money
5. **Receive your payout** in Mexican pesos (SPEI transfer) or USDT (Polygon network)

**DevEx Conditions:**
- **14-day holding period** from AXF purchase (anti-money laundering)
- Identity verification (**KYC**) required
- Buyback rate: approximately **70%** of AXF nominal value
- **Only applies to AXF received from P2P sales** — directly purchased AXF does not qualify for DevEx (you must circulate it through the economy first)

> **Important:** DevEx is not an "ATM." It is designed for players who create real value in the ecosystem (rare cards, well-bred Axolotitos, optimized boards). It is not a way to buy AXF and withdraw immediately.

## FRJ (Frijolitos) — The Game Currency

Frijolitos are the currency you actually **play with.** They are not crypto — they exist only in the game's database. They have no real-money value and **cannot be withdrawn** by design. This is what allows Axolotto to operate as a skill contest rather than a casino.

### How to Earn FRJ (For Free)

There are many ways to earn Frijolitos without spending a single peso:

#### 1. Winning Matches (CPU Mode)

| Difficulty | Entry Fee | Prize per Win |
|---|---|---|
| Rookies (beginner) | 25 FRJ | 85 FRJ (net gain: +60) |
| Champions (expert) | 100 FRJ | 400 FRJ (net gain: +300) |

Play against the CPU, win consistently, and accumulate FRJ. The average win rate for active players is around 30% — the house has a mathematical edge, as in any skill-based game of chance.

#### 2. Multiplayer Prizes

In multiplayer rooms, prizes come from the total pot collected from all players' entry fees:

- **Prize 1 (first Lotería):** 35% of the total pot, split among winners
- **Prize 2 (Full Board):** 55% of the total pot, split among those who complete their board
- **Jackpot:** 90% of the accumulated global pot (see [[22-jackpot]] for conditions)

The remaining 10% of the pot is split between the game treasury (5%) and the jackpot (5%).

#### 3. Daily Rewards — Lunar Cycle

Every day you log in you receive free FRJ. The amount grows throughout the week:

| Day of the week | Daily FRJ |
|---|---|
| Days 1-2 | 50 FRJ |
| Days 3-4 | 80 FRJ |
| Days 5-6 | 130 FRJ |
| Day 7 | Special bonus (free capsule + extra FRJ) |

The streak resets every Monday (weekly lunar cycle). See [[21-ciclo-lunar-y-recompensas]].

#### 4. Staking — Passive Income

You can stake your boards and Axolotitos to generate FRJ per hour, even when you are not playing. Rarer cards on your board produce more FRJ per hour:

| Card Rarity | FRJ per hour (per card) |
|---|---|
| Common | 0.05 |
| Rare | 0.15 |
| Epic | 0.40 |
| Legendary | 1.00 |

A full board of 16 Legendary cards at level 10 generates up to **35.20 FRJ per hour** (844.80 FRJ per day). Staking requires having played at least one match in the last 24 hours. See [[20-staking-ingresos-pasivos]].

#### 5. P2P Market — Sell to Other Players

Sell duplicate cards, boards you no longer use, or Axolotitos you bred to other players. You set the price in FRJ. See [[19-mercado-p2p]].

#### 6. VIP Daily Claim

Each VIP level includes a daily FRJ claim:

| VIP Level | Daily FRJ |
|---|---|
| Coral | 40 FRJ |
| Dorado | 100 FRJ |
| Axolite | 200 FRJ |

See [[15-vip-club]].

#### 7. Spectator Mode

You can watch multiplayer matches without playing and earn up to **10 FRJ per day** completely free. Great for learning strategies while generating some currency.

### What FRJ Buys

- **Match entry fees** — 25 FRJ (Rookies) or 100 FRJ (Champions)
- **Board creation** — 25 to 50 FRJ per new board
- **Gashapon capsules** — from 1,500 to 22,500 FRJ per pull
- **Consumables:**
  - Axolotito food: 30 to 150 FRJ
  - Anti-frost (Solvente): 200 FRJ
  - Energy drops: various prices
- **P2P Market purchases** — cards, boards, Axolotitos from other players
- **Cave expansion** — from 500 FRJ (basic level) to 60,000 FRJ (deep levels)
- **Card Melter** — 100 to 1,500 FRJ to destroy duplicates and receive materials

## Currency Exchange — The Banco de Algas

The **Banco de Algas** (Algae Bank) is the only way to convert AXF into FRJ. There is no reverse conversion (FRJ → AXF) — that requires going through the P2P market.

**AXF → FRJ Conversion Packages:**

| Package | AXF Cost | FRJ Received | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alga Común (Common Algae) | 10 AXF | 100 FRJ | — |
| Alga Abundante (Abundant Algae) | 50 AXF | 600 FRJ | +20% |
| Alga Imperial (Imperial Algae) | 100 AXF | 1,500 FRJ | +50% |
| Súper Carga (Super Charge) | 250 AXF | 4,000 FRJ | +60% |

The larger the package, the better the conversion rate. The Súper Carga package gives you 16 FRJ per AXF, compared to just 10 FRJ per AXF in the basic package.

**Recommendation for new players:** If you are buying AXF to convert to FRJ, the Alga Imperial package (100 AXF → 1,500 FRJ) is the best cost-benefit balance to start with.

## Why Two Currencies?

This is the most important question. The short answer: **legal compliance.**

In many countries (Mexico included), games where you bet real money to win real money are considered casinos and require special licenses. By separating the currencies:

1. **FRJ is the gameplay currency** — it has no real-money value, cannot be bought or withdrawn. Multiplayer matches only accept FRJ. This allows Axolotto to operate as a **skill contest** with virtual prizes, not as a gambling game with bets.

2. **AXF is the collectibles currency** — it represents ownership of digital assets (NFTs). Its value fluctuates in the P2P market based on supply and demand for in-game items, not based on match results.

3. **The code enforces it** — any attempt to use AXF in a multiplayer room is rejected with error **HTTP 402**. The restriction is programmed at the server level and cannot be bypassed.

4. **The real-money path is indirect** — to withdraw money you must: play well → earn FRJ → create/obtain valuable assets → sell them on the P2P market → receive AXF from another player → request DevEx. This takes time, skill, and effort. It is not a disguised casino.

## Strategy — Which Currency Should I Focus On?

### If You Are Free-to-Play (F2P)

Your world is **Frijolitos.** You do not need AXF for anything essential:
- Play CPU matches to earn FRJ
- Claim your daily Lunar Cycle rewards without fail
- Stake your best boards for passive income
- Sell duplicate cards on the P2P Market
- Save FRJ for Gashapon capsules and cave expansion
- See the full guide: [[25-f2p-guia-gratis]]

### If You Invest Real Money

Buy **AXF** and use it wisely:
- **Best use:** Monthly VIP (passive benefits that accumulate) + Premium Sobrecitos
- **Efficient conversion:** Alga Imperial or Súper Carga to maximize FRJ per AXF
- **Long-term investment:** Webitos to breed rare Axolotitos and sell them on the P2P market
- **Not recommended:** Spending AXF on small speed-ups or basic consumables (you waste the premium value)

### If You Want to Withdraw Money (DevEx)

This is the "professional" player's path:
1. Master the game — consistent wins
2. Breed Axolotitos with high stats and desirable natures
3. Build boards optimized for staking
4. Sell your assets on the P2P Market for AXF
5. Request DevEx when you have a cushion of AXF from P2P sales

## Historical Note

The currency names changed in June 2026 to better align with the game's identity:

- **AXG (Axogemas)** was renamed to **AXF (Axofichas)**
- **GAL (Gemas Alga)** was renamed to **FRJ (Frijolitos)**

If you see "AXG" or "GAL" in an old interface, screenshot, or YouTube video, they are exactly the same as AXF and FRJ — only the name changed. The blockchain contracts keep their original names (`Axogema.sol`, `GemaAlga.sol`) due to immutability.
