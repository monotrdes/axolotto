## English

### What is Axolotto?

Axolotto is a **Mexican Lotería game set in a Web3 metaverse**, built within a digital recreation of the Xochimilco canals. You play on 4x4 boards, assign stats to your axolotitos, open booster packs, incubate eggs, compete in multiplayer tournaments, and earn real cryptocurrency rewards (AXF and FRJ) while a mysterious **Gritón** (Caller) sings the cards from the depths of the cenote.

### Digital Xochimilco — The Canals That Never Sleep

Picture the Xochimilco canals at night, lit by neon. The water reflects pink, violet, and gold light. Trajineras (traditional flat-bottomed boats) drift silently between digital chinampas (floating gardens). The sky is always starry, but the stars are *code*.

At the heart of it all lies **El Cenote** — a massive flooded cave, the game's central hub. This is where the Gritón calls the cards, where eggs hatch, and where players gather. Light rays pierce the water from the surface. Bubbles rise slowly. Echoes bounce off stone walls.

The aesthetic is **dark neon**: near-black backgrounds with neon pink (`#E4007C`), teal, gold, and violet highlights. It is a 2.5D world with depth layers — foreground elements move faster than distant ones, bringing the scene to life like an animated paper diorama.

### Did You Know? The Real Axolotl

Axolotls (*Ambystoma mexicanum*) are real salamanders native to the Xochimilco canals in Mexico City. They are **critically endangered** — fewer than 1,000 remain in the wild. Yet millions thrive in captivity as popular aquarium pets and laboratory research subjects.

Why labs? Because axolotls possess an extraordinary ability: **they can regenerate entire limbs, internal organs, and even portions of their brain and spinal cord**. Scientists study them to unlock regeneration in humans.

Their name comes from **Xolotl**, the Aztec god of fire, lightning, and death — twin brother of Quetzalcoatl. According to legend, Xolotl transformed into an axolotl to hide and avoid being sacrificed.

Axolotto celebrates this heritage. Every axolotito in the game is a unique NFT with digital DNA determining its appearance and abilities.

### The Lotería — A 200+ Year Tradition

Mexican Lotería is a traditional card game, similar to bingo, played at fairs, family gatherings, and parties for over **two centuries**. A deck of 54 cards features iconic illustrations: El Corazón (The Heart), La Sirena (The Mermaid), El Borracho (The Drunkard), La Muerte (Death), El Sol (The Sun).

In a traditional game, a **cantador** (caller or "gritón") draws cards at random and sings their names — sometimes with rhymes, puns, or jokes. Players mark their boards with beans (frijolitos). The first to complete a line or full board shouts **"¡Lotería!"** and wins.

Axolotto digitizes this tradition while preserving its essence: the same 54 cards, the same board system, the same victory cry. But it adds layers of strategy: **stat selection, custom board building, budget management, and real Web3 economics**.

> Axolotto is a **skill contest** (concurso de destreza), not a casino. Player skill matters: choosing the right stats, building efficient boards, and managing your budget separates an expert from a beginner. Luck helps, but strategy decides.

### The Gritón — The Voice of the Cenote

The **Gritón** is Axolotto's official caller. A mysterious figure whose face no one has ever seen. His voice thunders through the cenote as he announces each card. Some say he is an ancient axolotl awakened from a thousand-year slumber beneath the canals. Others say he is simply… a very loud señor.

The Gritón does more than call cards — he welcomes new players and presides over special ceremonies like the hatching of rare eggs. Listen closely, because his rhymes sometimes hide clues.

### Cenote Climate — Real-Time Day and Night

The cenote follows a day-night cycle matching real Xochimilco conditions. The game has **four climate phases**:

- **Dawn** 🌅 — Soft golden light. Eggs incubated at dawn complete their cycle 10% faster.
- **Morning** ☀️ — Bright and warm. Higher player activity.
- **Afternoon** 🌤️ — Soft orange light. Special events often occur during this window.
- **Night** 🌙 — Darkness with neon flashes. Eggs retain heat longer at night, positively affecting incubation.

Heat retention varies by time of day: eggs incubated at night hold their internal temperature longer than midday ones. This is not merely cosmetic — it affects hatching speed and can influence which traits your axolotito inherits.
