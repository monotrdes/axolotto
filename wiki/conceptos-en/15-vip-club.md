# VIP Club — Premium Membership

The **Axolotto VIP Club** is a monthly (30-day) premium subscription system offering exclusive benefits across the entire platform: daily FRJ generation, store discounts, free gashapon capsules, extra slots, and more. All prices are paid in **AXF (Axofichas)**. It is not pay-to-win — everything the VIP grants can also be earned with FRJ, just more slowly.

## Break-Even Analysis

## Daily Claim Mechanic

VIP daily FRJ does **NOT auto-credit** — you must log in and claim it manually each day. This is intentional — we want you visiting the Nido every day.

- **Maximum accumulation**: 2 days. If you miss claiming today, today's FRJ carries over to tomorrow.
- **Expiration**: On the third day without claiming, the oldest batch expires and is lost.
- **Animation**: When claiming, FRJ coins fly to your counter with a satisfying animation. A small moment of daily joy.
- **Tip**: Log in for at least 1 minute a day to claim your FRJ. It is the most valuable VIP benefit.

## VIP Streak Rewards (Permanent!)

Maintaining your VIP subscription active for consecutive months unlocks **permanent** rewards that you keep even if your VIP later expires.

| Consecutive months | Reward | Type |
|--------------------|--------|------|
| **3 months** | "Constante" Badge | Permanent |
| **6 months** | "Veteran" cosmetic frame | Permanent |
| **12 months** | "Axolotto Original" Title + 1 FREE Astral Webito | Permanent |
| **24 months** | "Legend of the Nido" frame — rarest in the game | Permanent |

### Streak tracking

- The streak is tracked as `vip_streak_months` in your profile.
- To maintain the streak, you must renew your VIP within a **27 to 63 day window** from the previous activation.
- If you let **more than 63 days** pass without an active VIP, the streak **resets to zero**.
- Rewards already earned are yours forever, even if the streak resets.

## Upgrade Mechanic

If you already have an active VIP tier and want to upgrade to a higher one, the system applies a **proportional credit** for the unused days of your current plan.

### Formula

```
credit = (current_plan_price / 30) × days_remaining
final_price = new_plan_price - credit
```

### Example

You have **Coral (400 AXF)** with **15 days remaining**. You want to upgrade to **Dorado (600 AXF)**.

- Credit: (400 / 30) × 15 = **200 AXF**
- Final price: 600 - 200 = **400 AXF**

You only pay 400 AXF for the upgrade, and your 15 remaining days are not wasted.

### Important upgrade notes

- The **first activation** of each tier (the first time you buy Coral, Dorado, or Axolite) always grants welcome bonuses: extra FRJ and boosters.
- If you already received the Dorado welcome bonus in the past, a later upgrade to Dorado does not grant it again.
- **Monthly capsules** are delivered at the time of purchase or renewal. When upgrading, you receive the new tier's capsules immediately (prorated for remaining days).

## What Happens When VIP Expires

When your VIP subscription ends, you lose nothing permanently, but extra benefits are deactivated:

### Immediate effects upon expiry

- **Daily FRJ**: You stop receiving the daily bonus.
- **Discounts**: Prices return to base (no discount).
- **P2P commissions**: Return to the standard rate (5%).
- **Monthly capsules**: No longer delivered until renewal.

### Extra slots (freeze effect)

- **Extra board slots**: The newest boards (those occupying the extra slots) get **frozen** and return HTTP 423 Locked if you try to access them. They are not deleted — upon renewal, they unfreeze automatically.
- **Extra Axolotito slot (Axolite)**: The most recent Axolotito gets frozen. If it was your main Axolotito, the system automatically reassigns the next available one.

### Grace period

- You have a **7-day grace period** after expiry.
- You will receive notifications at **7, 3, and 1 days** before your VIP expires.
- During the grace period, some minor benefits may persist (such as the visual frame).
- Your Axolotitos and boards **do not disappear** — they are just locked until renewal.

## Auto-Renewal

The auto-renewal system lets you keep your VIP active without worrying about manual renewal.

- **Activation**: ON/OFF toggle in the VIP section of your Profile.
- **How it works**: At the end of your 30-day period, the system automatically deducts the cost in AXF from your wallet and renews for another 30 days.
- **Requirement**: You must have sufficient AXF balance at the time of charge.
- **If balance is insufficient**: Auto-renewal fails, you receive a notification, and your VIP expires normally (with the 7-day grace period).
- **Recommendation**: Keep it ON if you play regularly. It is a "set and forget" that ensures you never lose your VIP streak due to forgetfulness.

## Which VIP Is Right for You? (Player Guide)

### Casual Player (a few games per week)
**Choose Coral.** For 400 AXF/month (~$40 USD), you get 1,200 FRJ, 5% discount, and 2 Bronze capsules. Total value (~520 AXF) exceeds the cost. Ideal if you play on weekends or some afternoons.

### Active Player (daily player)
**Choose Dorado.** For 600 AXF/month (~$60 USD), you get 3,000 FRJ, 12% discount, +1 board slot, and 4 capsules. This is the most popular tier because it offers the **best value/AXF ratio** of the three. Total value (~1,390 AXF) doubles the investment.

### Hardcore Player (many hours per day)
**Choose Axolite.** For 1,800 AXF/month (~$180 USD), you get the full package: 6,000 FRJ, 20% discount, 7 capsules including 1 Gold, +2 board slots, +1 Axolotito slot, 15% Multiplayer discount, and +5% jackpot bonus. Estimated total value (~4,300-4,900 AXF) triples the investment if you leverage all benefits.

**You don't need VIP.** Everything the VIP Club offers can also be obtained without spending real AXF, just more slowly. Axolotto is designed so F2P and VIP players coexist in the same ecosystem.

## FAQ

**Can I switch VIP tiers without losing my remaining days?**
Yes. The upgrade system applies a proportional credit for unused days. You only pay the difference.

**Are streak rewards lost if my VIP expires?**
No. Streak rewards (badges, frames, titles, Astral Webito) are **permanent**. If your streak resets, you do not lose what you already earned.

**Can I gift VIP to another player?**
Not currently. VIP is personal and non-transferable. We are evaluating adding VIP gift cards in the future.

**Can VIP be paid with FRJ?**
No. VIP is paid exclusively with AXF. However, VIP benefits generate FRJ that you can use in-game.

**What if I have VIP and the server goes down on a day I didn't claim?**
The daily claim system has a 2-day accumulation window precisely to cover contingencies. If the server is down for more than 2 days, contact support.
