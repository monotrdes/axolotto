## English

### What is a Board (Tabla)?

The **board** is your primary game tool in Axolotto. It is a **4×4** grid holding exactly **16 cards** from your personal collection. Each board is a **unique ERC-721 NFT** — you own it, you can sell it, rent it, level it up, and show it off.

No board means no game. You need **at least 1 board** to sit down and play when the Gritón starts calling cards.

**Card POSITION matters.** Placing El Corazón in the corner is not the same as placing it in the center. Certain win patterns — such as corners, crosses, or 2×2 blocks — require specific grid positions. Building your board well is the difference between shouting ¡Lotería! and watching someone else take the prize.

> Think of your board as a squad of 16 soldiers. Formation decides the battle.

---

### Creating a Board

Create boards from the **Sala** (Game Room) under "Mis Tablas" (My Boards). Two methods available:

#### Random (Aleatorio) — 25 FRJ
The system randomly selects 16 cards from your collection and places them automatically. Quick, cheap, and sometimes chaos works. Ideal when:
- You are starting out and do not have many cards to choose from
- You want to test your luck without investing much
- You need an extra board fast for staking or rental

#### Manual Design (Diseño) — 50 FRJ
The **Board Editor** gives you full control. Drag and drop each card into the exact grid position you want. You see the full board while you build. This method is **strategically superior** because you can:
- Place your best cards in positions that form multiple win patterns
- Distribute rarities to cover rows, columns, and diagonals
- Avoid blind spots — zones with no pattern coverage
- Create specialized boards (anti-Rookie, anti-Champion, balanced)

Veteran tip: **always manually design your competitive boards**. The extra 25 FRJ pays for itself with your first win.

### Board Types — Tiers

Not all boards are created equal. The **type** determines base properties and which game modes you can play:

| Type | Game Cost | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Clásica** (Classic) | 10 AXF | The basic board. Perfect for learning and farming FRJ in easy matches. Your first board will always be Clásica. |
| **Suerte** (Luck) | 50 AXF | Higher reward multiplier. For players who trust their strategy and want serious earnings. |
| **Plasma** | 150 AXF | High-risk board. Huge prizes but tougher matches. Only for experts with a solid bankroll. |
| **Cósmica** (Cosmic) | 200 AXF | The ultimate tier. The best multipliers in the game. Matches against the hardest opponents. If you win with a Cósmica, the Gritón will remember your name. |

Each board keeps its type for life. You cannot "upgrade" a Clásica to Suerte — but you can own multiple boards of different tiers and choose which one to use depending on the occasion.

### Board Stats

Every board accumulates stats that reflect its history and performance. These are your calling card before buyers and renters:

#### Level
Your board earns **XP** every match you play with it. The formula is the same as for axolotitos: `level × 100 XP` to reach the next level. A level 5 board needs 500 XP to hit level 6.

Leveling up **improves staking rates** (you generate more passive FRJ) and **increases the board's P2P value**.

#### CSR — Win Rate (Coeficiente de Suerte y Rendimiento)
A **0 to 100** score measuring how well your board performs. It combines:
- Your win percentage with this board
- The difficulty of opponents you have beaten
- The board tier (winning with a Clásica against Champions weighs more)

A CSR of 80+ is excellent. 95+ is otherworldly. Boards with high CSR rent and sell for much more.

#### Suerte Tag (Luck Tag)
Two fun flavor tags automatically assigned by the system:
- **Luckiest Board**: the board with the best recent win streak (highest percentage over the last 20 games)
- **Unluckiest Board**: the board with the worst streak — we all have one

These tags are just for fun, but buyers do look at them.

#### Lifetime Record
- **Total Games**: how many matches you have played with this board
- **Total Wins**: how many times you have shouted ¡Lotería! with it
- **Current Streak**: consecutive wins (or consecutive losses)

### XP from Matches — What You Earn

Your board's XP depends on match type and outcome:

#### CPU Matches

| Result | Rookies (10 AXF) | Champions (50 AXF) |
|---|---|---|
| **Win** | 25 XP | 60 XP |
| **Loss** | 8 XP | 15 XP |

Even losing teaches you something. Your boards always progress, just slower.

#### Multiplayer Matches

| Prize | Bonus XP |
|---|---|
| **Prize 1** (main winner) | +15 XP |
| **Prize 2** (second place) | +45 XP |

#### 10× Multiplier
When you activate the **10× multiplier**, base XP is multiplied by **√10 ≈ 3.16×**. It is not a direct 10× to keep progression balanced, but it is still a significant acceleration.

### Board Staking — Passive Income

Your boards **generate FRJ automatically** while you are not using them to play. This is Axolotto's passive staking system: your boards "work" for you.

#### How much does each board generate?

The generation rate depends on two factors:
1. **Card rarity** on the board — rarer cards = more FRJ/hour
2. **Board level** — higher level = better base rate

#### Play-to-Stake Rule
To keep staking active, you must play **at least 1 game every 24 hours**. If 24 hours pass without playing, your boards stop generating FRJ until you play again. This rule exists so staking rewards active participation, not eternal free income.

#### Accumulation Cap — 24 Hours
Your boards accumulate FRJ for a maximum of **24 hours**. If you do not claim before that limit, accumulation stops. **Claim your FRJ regularly** — set a reminder if you tend to forget.

#### Foil Cards — Yield Bonus
Each **foil card** (shiny holographic variant) on your board adds a **+5% yield bonus** to staking. With 16 foil cards, you can reach up to **+80%** bonus. Foil cards are rare but they turn your board into an FRJ-generating machine.

> Strategy: if you have a board solely for staking, fill it with your highest-rarity foil cards. Position matters less — only rarity and foil status count.

### Board Rental (Scholarship)

Got a powerful board you are not using full-time? **Rent it out.** Do not have a competitive board but want to play in higher tiers? **Rent one.**

#### As Owner (Landlord)
1. List your board for rent from "Mis Tablas"
2. Set a **FRJ price per 24 hours** of rental
3. Define the **owner share percentage** of winnings when the renter wins
4. You earn FRJ **risk-free** — you get paid even if the renter loses every game
5. The board remains yours. The renter only uses it, they cannot modify it

#### As Renter (Scholar)
- **Try before you buy**: rent a board to see if you like it before investing in building a similar one
- **Play in higher tiers**: rent a Cósmica or Plasma even if you lack the cards to build your own
- **Learn from the best**: study how top players position their cards
- You can rent boards directly from the **Rankings** leaderboard — the best boards are often available for rent

> The scholarship system makes Axolotto accessible to everyone. You do not need to be a whale to play Cósmica — you just need a good owner who trusts you.

### P2P Board Sales

Sell entire boards on the **P2P Marketplace** (Tianguis). A sale includes:
- The board as an ERC-721 NFT (if both parties have linked wallets)
- All 16 cards it contains
- Its full history: level, CSR, record, luck tags

#### Market Fee

| VIP Tier | Fee |
|---|---|
| No VIP | 5% |
| VIP Dorado | 4% |
| VIP Axolite | 3% |
| VIP Leyenda | 1.5% |

A level 20 board with 90+ CSR and a winning streak can be worth thousands of FRJ. Building a good board is an investment that pays off generously.

### Board Dismantling

Sometimes you need to start fresh. You have two options:

#### Basic Dismantling — 50 FRJ
Destroys the board. **1 random card is lost forever.** The other **15 cards return to your inventory.** It is a gamble — make sure you have no irreplaceable cards on that board before dismantling.

#### Glue Solvent (Pegamento Solvente) — 120 FRJ
A special consumable that dissolves the board without damaging the cards. **All 16 cards return intact** to your inventory. Worth every FRJ if you have foil cards, legendaries, or cards of sentimental value.

> Golden rule: if your board has even 1 foil or legendary card, use Glue Solvent. 120 FRJ is nothing compared to losing a card worth 10,000+ FRJ.

### Board Strategy — Build Like a Pro

#### Rule #1: Pattern Coverage
The goal is not just having 16 powerful cards. It is **covering as many win patterns as possible.** Each position on the 4×4 grid belongs to multiple patterns:
- **4 rows** (horizontal)
- **4 columns** (vertical)
- **2 diagonals** (main and anti-diagonal)
- **4 corners** (the corner clusters)
- **9 cuadritos** (2×2 blocks)
- **1 central square** (the 4 center cells)

Every card you place participates in 2–4 patterns simultaneously. A poorly placed card is a missed opportunity.

#### Rule #2: Distribute Your Rarities
- **Legendaries and Epics** in high-traffic positions (center, corners, main diagonal)
- **Rares** in rows and columns to ensure line coverage
- **Commons** in positions that only participate in 1–2 patterns

Do not stack your best cards in a single row. If that row does not hit, you lose.

#### Rule #3: Specialize by Game Mode

**For Rookies (easy matches):**
- Prioritize **line** coverage (rows and columns) — the most common patterns
- You do not need perfect cuadrito coverage; Rookies rarely complete complex patterns
- A balanced board with 2–3 rare cards per line is enough

**For Champions (hard matches):**
- You need **2×2 cuadrito** and **corner** coverage in addition to lines
- Champion opponents also hunt for complex patterns — you are competing for the same ones
- Invest in foil and legendary cards for these boards
- A cuadrito-specialized board can be devastating in Champions

#### Rule #4: Staking Board vs Game Board
Keep at least **two boards with different purposes:**
- **Game board**: optimized for winning matches (strategic positions, versatile cards)
- **Staking board**: optimized for generating FRJ (maximum rarity, maximum foil — level and position matter less)

#### Rule #5: CSR Is Your Resume
Every win improves your CSR. Every loss lowers it. A high CSR means:
- Your board rents faster and for more FRJ
- Your P2P sale price skyrockets
- Other players recognize you on the Rankings leaderboard

Protect your CSR. If you are on a losing streak, switch to another board so you do not damage your main board's record.
