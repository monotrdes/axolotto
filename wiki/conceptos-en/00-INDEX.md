## English

Welcome to the **Axolotto Concept Wiki** — a player-friendly encyclopedia of everything in the Axolotto universe. Think of it as the Pokédex for Axolotto.

**Axolotto** is a Web3 Mexican Lotería game set in **Xochimilco Digital**. You collect Axolotitos (NFT digital pets), build Lotería boards, compete in multiplayer rooms, and earn rewards in a dual-currency economy.
