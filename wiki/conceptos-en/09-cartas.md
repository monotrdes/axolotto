## English

**Lotería Cards** are the beating heart of Axolotto. If the game were a body, the cards would be the blood — they flow through every corner of the ecosystem, from the store to the board, from the forge to the P2P market. No cards means no boards, no boards means no game, and no game… well, why did you come?

There are 54 unique cards based on traditional Mexican Lotería: **El Gallo** (The Rooster), **La Dama** (The Lady), **El Diablito** (The Little Devil), **La Luna** (The Moon), **El Sol** (The Sun), **El Corazón** (The Heart), **La Mano** (The Hand), **La Rosa** (The Rose), **El Pescado** (The Fish), **La Sirena** (The Mermaid), **La Muerte** (Death), **El Árbol** (The Tree), **El Mundo** (The World)… each with its own original illustration blending classic Mexican vintage aesthetics with a cyberpunk-axolotl twist.

Each card is an **ERC-1155** token, meaning multiple copies of the same card can exist in circulation. Unlike Axolotitos (ERC-721), these aren't one-of-a-kind — the magic here lives in the rarity, the shine, and the edition.

## Rarity System

Not all cards are created equal. When you open a booster, the universe decides your fate according to this five-tier system:

| Rarity | Border Color | Difficulty | Description |
|--------|-------------|------------|-------------|
| **Común** (Common) | Subtle gray | Base | You see them everywhere. Daily bread. Perfect for starting out and melting in the forge. |
| **Rara** (Uncommon) | Teal | Moderate | Now we're talking. A bit harder to pull, they shine with a teal-cyan border. |
| **Épica** (Rare) | Bright cyan | Hard | These demand attention. The electric cyan border screams "look at me." Collecting all of them is an achievement. |
| **Legendaria** (Epic) | Violet | Very hard | The violet border commands respect. When one drops from a booster, the room lights up. |
| **Mítica** (Legendary) | Gold with glow | Extremely rare | The *chase cards*. Gold border with animated glow. If you pull a Mítica, screenshot it — it doesn't happen often. The holy grail of any collection. |

The rarity system isn't just cosmetic — it affects your board's value in-game, the fragments you earn from melting, and of course, P2P market prices.

## Special Variants

Because a card isn't just about rarity. It can also have… *style*.

### Brillante (Foil / Shiny)

The **Foil** variant applies an animated rainbow gradient across the card's artwork. It's not a static PNG — the card *lives*, reflecting shifting colors like a mesmerizing oil slick.

- **Drop rate**: ~3% from foil boosters, or as a special Gashapon drop (depending on capsule tier).
- **In-game effect**: Each foil card on your board grants **+5% FRJ yield** per game, stacking up to **+80%** (16 foil cards on a 4x4 board). If you fill a full board with foil… you're a legend.
- **Market value**: Extremely high. Collectors will pay anything for foils of their favorite cards. A foil Mítica is basically a unicorn.

### Primera Edición (First Edition)

Marked with a gold star ⭐ in the top corner. These are the **first minted copies** of each card — the ones that dropped during Fase 1 (Genesis).

- **Collector value**: Significantly higher than an equivalent card without the star.
- **Status**: If you have a ⭐, you were there first. That commands respect.
- **Limited supply**: Only units minted in Fase 1 get the mark. Once the phase sells out, no more are produced.

### Foil + First Edition

The **ultimate combination**. A card that is both Foil AND First Edition is the rarest object in the card ecosystem. If you have one, frame it. If you have several, open a museum.

## The 3 Supply Phases

Card supply is not infinite. The system progresses through three global phases that determine how many units have been issued and what boosters cost.

### Fase 1 — Genesis (First Edition)

- **Units**: First **420** units of each themed booster.
- **Shiny chance**: 15% per individual card.
- **Marking**: All cards from this phase get the ⭐ First Edition star.
- **Status**: The early adopter phase. When it sells out, it's gone forever.

### Fase 2 — Expansion (Unlimited)

- **Units**: Next **1,260** units of each themed booster.
- **Shiny chance**: 20% chance that **one card in the pack** is shiny (not per card — one guaranteed slot with a 20% activation rate).
- **No star**: First Edition marking is no longer applied.
- **Status**: The main phase. Most players start here.

### Fase 3 — Retail

- **Units**: Final **2,520** units of each booster type.
- **Pricing**: Boosters become more expensive in this phase.
- **Status**: The final stretch of supply. When Fase 3 sells out, that booster type stops being sold in the store.

**Automatic transition**: When a phase sells out globally (all units sold), the system advances to the next phase without manual intervention. There's no warning — one day the booster costs 6 AXF, and the next morning it's Fase 2 at 10 AXF.

## How to Get Cards

Multiple paths lead to your collection. Not all of them require opening your wallet.

### 1. Boosters (Sobres) — The Store

The classic way. Go to the store, buy a booster, open it, and pray to the RNG gods. Each booster contains **7 cards**.

Booster types:

| Booster Type | Card Pool | Theme |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| **Pure (Puro)** | All 54 cards | No filter — anything can drop. The classic booster. |
| **Fiesta** | 18 cards | Celebration collection: El Gallo, La Dama, El Paraguas, El Sol, La Luna, and more — festive cards. |
| **Nido (Nest)** | 18 cards | Nature collection: El Árbol, La Rosa, El Pescado, La Sirena, El Camarón, El Pájaro — flora and fauna. |
| **Cosmos** | 18 cards | Mystical collection: La Muerte, El Diablito, La Estrella, El Mundo, La Luna — cards from beyond. |
| **Foil (Brillante)** | 54 cards, guaranteed foil | Every single one of the 7 cards is a guaranteed foil. The premium booster. |

### 2. Prices by Phase

| Type | Fase 1 (AXF + FRJ) | Fase 2 (AXF + FRJ) | Fase 3 (AXF + FRJ) |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Puro** | 6 AXF + 60 FRJ | 10 AXF + 100 FRJ | 15 AXF + 150 FRJ |
| **Fiesta** | 10 AXF + 100 FRJ | 15 AXF + 150 FRJ | 20 AXF + 200 FRJ |
| **Nido** | 10 AXF + 100 FRJ | 15 AXF + 150 FRJ | 20 AXF + 200 FRJ |
| **Cosmos** | 10 AXF + 100 FRJ | 15 AXF + 150 FRJ | 20 AXF + 200 FRJ |
| **Foil** | 80 AXF + 800 FRJ | — | — |

> **Note**: The Foil booster is capped at **100 units per month globally**. It only exists in Fase 1. When they're announced, they sell out in minutes. Enable notifications.

### 3. Gashapon — Capsules

Gashapon capsule machines can drop cards as part of their reward pool. Probabilities vary:

- **Basic tier**: ~28% chance a capsule contains at least one card.
- **Premium tier**: ~43% chance.
- **VIP tier**: Up to 50%, with a chance at high rarities and foils.

See [[14-capsulas-gashapon]] for full details.

### 4. P2P Market

Got a duplicate La Sirena foil? Desperately need El Corazón to complete your collection? The P2P market is your place. Buy and sell cards directly with other players, in AXF or FRJ. Prices are set by the community.

See [[13-tienda-y-tianguis]] for more details.

### 5. Forge and Foundry (Card Melter)

Too many duplicates? The forge is your way out. Turn cards you don't need into something better. See [[18-forja-y-fundicion]] for the complete system.

Quick melting summary:

| Action | Requirement | Cost | Result |
|--------|------------|------|--------|
| **Melt Common** | 5 identical Common cards (non-foil, non-⭐) | 100 FRJ | 1 random Rare card + 10 fragments |
| **Melt Rare** | 5 identical Rare cards (non-foil, non-⭐) | 250 FRJ | 1 random Epic card + 10 fragments |
| **Melt Epic** | 5 identical Epic cards (non-foil, non-⭐) | 1,500 FRJ | 1 random Legendary card + 10 fragments |

- **Legendary and Mítica cards cannot be melted** — once they reach that tier, they stay.
- Each melt generates **10 fragments** (common, rare, epic, or legendary as appropriate). Fragments are used to forge specific cards.
- Foil and First Edition cards **cannot be used as melting material**. They are protected. If you try to melt a foil, the system basically asks "are you crazy?" (okay, not literally, but close).

### 6. VIP Capsules

If you have a VIP membership, you receive free monthly capsules that can contain cards, including high rarities. It's one of the most valued VIP club perks.

## Boards: From Cards to Gameplay

Cards aren't just for collecting — they're the raw material for building **Lotería boards**. A board is a 4x4 grid (16 cards) that you use to play.

- Each board is registered on-chain as a unique token (ERC-721 TablasLoteria).
- Cards placed on your board aren't consumed — they're simply "assigned" to that board. You can reuse them on other boards.
- The foil bonus (+5% FRJ per foil card on the board) applies during gameplay.
- Different card combinations can unlock hidden achievements.

See [[10-tablas]] for the complete board-building system.

## Collector Tips

- **Use inventory filters**: Filter by rarity, by foil, by "I don't have it." The inventory tells you exactly how many you're missing to complete the base collection.
- **Never melt foils or ⭐**: Ever. Even if you somehow have 20 duplicate foil El Gallo cards (which won't happen), they're worth more on the market than turned into a random Rare.
- **P2P market to finish**: When you're 2 or 3 cards away from a complete collection, the P2P market is more efficient than continuing to open boosters and pray.
- **Themed boosters for hunting**: If you're after a specific card, a themed booster shrinks the pool from 54 to 18, tripling your odds.
- **Enable foil notifications**: Foil boosters disappear fast. Set up alerts so you don't miss out.
- **Patience pays**: A complete foil collection isn't built in a week. Enjoy the journey, celebrate every foil that drops, and show off your finds in the community.
