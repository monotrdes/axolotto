# The Cenote — Cave Expansion System

The Cenote is your personal underwater sanctuary in the heart of Xochimilco Digital. This is not just a cave: it is your home, your operations center, and the backbone of your progression in Axolotto. Here you incubate Webitos, house your axolotitos, receive visitors, decorate, and unlock permanent multipliers that transform your entire economy.

## What is the Cenote?

Picture an immersive 2.5D underwater scene: depth layers fading into a mystical blue, light rays piercing the surface, bubbles slowly ascending, and your axolotitos swimming freely among rock formations covered in glowing moss. That is The Cenote.

- **Personal sanctuary**: Your private space in Xochimilco Digital, visible to other players who can visit and leave likes.
- **7 incubation slots**: Aquatic nests where you deposit Webitos to hatch into axolotitos.
- **NOT directly purchasable**: Each expansion requires completing an achievement first, then paying the FRJ cost + waiting out the excavation timer.
- **Each level unlocks**: More axolotito spots, decoration slots, table seats, staking slots, and economic multipliers.

> The cave is the main progression path. Each level makes you economically stronger. It is not cosmetic — it is strategic.

## Excavation Mechanic

Expanding the Cenote is not instant: each level requires **real-time excavation**. Your main axolotito digs through the cave while the timer runs.

| Level | Excavation Time |
|-------|-----------------|
| 1 → 2 | 2 hours |
| 2 → 3 | 6 hours |
| 3 → 4 | 12 hours |
| 4 → 5 | 24 hours |
| 5 → 6 | 36 hours |
| 6 → 7 | 48 hours |
| 7 → 8 | 72 hours |

- **Real-time**: The timer counts down whether you are in the game or not. Start the excavation and come back when it finishes.
- **Acceleration with AXF**: You can speed up the excavation by paying **4 AXF per remaining hour** (rounded up). Example: 11 hours remaining = 44 AXF.
- **⚠️ WARNING**: If you accelerate an expansion, you **lose the egg reward** for that level. Those eggs are extremely valuable — think twice before accelerating.
- **Level-up celebration**: When excavation completes naturally, a cinematic overlay triggers, revealing the new level name, design, and functionality.

> **Strategy**: Start long excavations (24h+) before going to sleep or when you know you will not play for a while. Patience pays with free eggs.

## Staking Slots from Cave

Staking is a passive income mechanic: you leave your axolotitos generating FRJ while you are away. Your cave level determines how many you can stake simultaneously.

- **Formula**: `cave_level + 1 = staking slots`
- Level 1: 2 slots. Level 8: 9 slots.
- Each staked axolotito generates passive FRJ per hour.
- While an axolotito is staked, it cannot be used in games or displayed in the cave.

> With 9 slots at level 8, you can run a full staking ecosystem generating income 24/7.

## Cave Decorations

Your Cenote is your canvas. As you expand, you unlock decoration slots to customize every corner.

- **4 categories**: FLOOR, WALL, WATER, SPECIAL
- **Slots**: 2 at level 1, +2 per level (max 16 at level 8)
- **2 minigames**:
  - **Wishing Well**: Toss 20 FRJ and make a wish (random reward).
  - **Arcade**: Play for 1 AXF (skill-based minigame).
- **Exhibition**: Display your star axolotito and trophies.
- **Visitors**: Other players can visit your Cenote and leave likes.

## Progression Strategy

Not all levels are equally important. This is the recommended priority order:

1. **Level 3 ASAP** — Unlocks table hosting and the extra starting card. The first major power spike.
2. **Level 5 next** — The extra incubation slot massively accelerates your breeding. More axolotitos = more staking = more FRJ.
3. **Level 7 after that** — The free monthly Foil booster is brutal passive income. And the free ASTral Webito is worth 3,000 AXF.
4. **Level 8 as the final goal** — The +10% AXF multiplier is the ultimate endgame reward.

### Key Tips

- **Save FRJ for expansions**: These are permanent upgrades. Every level pays for itself over time.
- **VIP transforms the experience**: 50% less cost and 50% less time is an enormous advantage.
- **Never accelerate an excavation**: The free eggs, especially the ASTral ones, are worth far more than the acceleration cost.
- **Start excavations before breaks**: If you are going to sleep or work, start the excavation and let time do its work.
- **The Level 7 ASTral Webito** is one of the most valuable items in the ecosystem: 3,000 AXF. Do not lose it by accelerating.
