## English

# Cave Decoration and Minigames

Your Cenote is more than a functional space: it is your canvas, your gallery, your private arcade, and your calling card to the community. Decorations transform a bare cave into a unique sanctuary that reflects your personality, attracts visitors, and provides real gameplay bonuses. And when you need a break from the lottery, two minigames await you inside your own cave.

## What is Cave Decoration?

Customizing your Cenote with decorative items is one of the most creative and social mechanics in Axolotto. It is not purely cosmetic: many decorations provide bonuses that boost your economy and axolotito efficiency.

- **Personalize your Cenote**: Choose from dozens of decorative items to make your cave unique.
- **Visitable by other players**: Your cave appears in public listings. Other players can enter, explore, and leave likes.
- **Gameplay bonuses**: Some decorations improve focus recovery, FRJ staking, or Webito incubation.
- **Creative expression**: Mix categories, themes, and colors. Some players build themed caves (pirate, space, jungle, minimalist...).

> A well-decorated cave is not just pretty: it is more efficient. Bonuses stack and scale with your cave level and axolotito collection.

## Decoration Categories

Every decorative item belongs to one category and occupies a specific slot type. You cannot place a WALL item in a FLOOR slot.

| Category | Examples | Slot Type |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **FLOOR** (Piso) | Algae beds, statues, treasure chests, sand carpets | Floor slots |
| **WALL** (Pared) | Bio-luminescent moss, VIP banners, cave paintings, decorative stalactites | Wall slots |
| **WATER** (Agua) | Floating lilies, floating toys, bubble streams, decorative jellyfish | Water slots |
| **SPECIAL** | Coral jukebox, arcade cabinet, thermal heater (interactive) | Special slots |
| **EXHIBIT** | Display your star axolotito + earned trophies | Exhibit slots |

## Decor Slots by Cave Level

As you expand your Cenote, you unlock more decoration slots. More slots = more decorations = a more impressive cave.

- **Formula**: `cave_level x 2 = decoration slots`
- Level 1: 2 slots. Level 8: 16 slots maximum.
- Linear progression: +2 slots every level.

## Bonuses from Decorations

Some decorations provide real gameplay bonuses. These bonuses stack with each other.

| Bonus | Effect | Scales with... | Maximum |
|-------|--------|---------------|---------|
| **Focus Recovery Boost** | Your axolotitos recover focus faster | Cave level | — |
| **Staking FRJ Multiplier** | Increases FRJ generated by staked axolotitos | Active axolotito count | — |
| **Incubation Thermal Boost** | Keeps Webitos warmer, accelerating incubation | Fixed | **+20%** |

## Minigame 1 — Wishing Well (Pozo de los Deseos)

Located in your cave, the Wishing Well is an accessible and addictive luck-based minigame. You toss 20 FRJ into the well, make a wish, and the well decides your fate.

- **Cost**: 20 FRJ per attempt
- **Random prizes**: FRJ (sometimes more than you tossed!), decorative items, axolotito accessories — and occasionally nothing at all.
- **No daily limit**: Toss as many wishes as you want... while you have FRJ.
- **Gambling-lite fun**: Do not spend your rent money here. It is for entertainment, not getting rich.

> "The wishing well giveth, and the wishing well taketh away." — Axolotito proverb

## Minigame 2 — Underwater Arcade (Arcade Submarino)

The Underwater Arcade is a skill-based minigame installed in your cave. Unlike the Well, your skill determines the outcome here.

- **Cost**: 1 AXF per play
- **Local leaderboard**: Each cave has its own high-score table. Only games played on YOUR arcade are recorded.
- **Compete with visitors**: Other players can play in your arcade when they visit, and their scores appear on your leaderboard. Defend your top spot.
- **Purely for fun and bragging rights**: No direct economic rewards. The reward is seeing your name at the top of the leaderboard... and making sure visitors see it too.

> "Retro gaming, but wetter." — Underwater Arcade tagline

## Social Features

Your cave is a social space. Other players can visit you, explore your decorations, play in your arcade, and leave likes.

- **Visits**: Any player can visit your Cenote from the public cave listing.
- **Likes**: Visitors can leave likes on caves they enjoy.
- **Lobby visibility**: Better-decorated caves with more likes appear higher in game listings. Good decoration attracts players to your tables.
- **Exhibition**: The EXHIBIT slot displays your star axolotito and most prized trophies. It is your calling card.
- **Coral Jukebox**: A SPECIAL item that lets visitors hear ambient music in your cave.
- **Thermal Heater**: Another interactive SPECIAL item that benefits Webito incubation (+20% thermal bonus).

## Getting Decorations

There are multiple sources for decorative items. Some are more accessible than others.

| Source | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Gashapon capsules** | Most common source. Capsules can contain decorations from all categories. |
| **P2P Market** | Buy decorations from other players in the Tianguis. Some rare items are only found here. |
| **Special events** | Holiday seasons, tournaments, and community events grant exclusive decorations. |
| **VIP rewards** | VIP members receive exclusive decorations unobtainable elsewhere. |
| **Cave expansion** | Some cave levels unlock unique decorations as expansion rewards. |
