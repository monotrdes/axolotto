## General Overview

The official Axolotto Shop is called **El Tianguis**, named after the traditional Mexican open-air markets. It has that same vibrant, colorful atmosphere: stalls full of merchandise, prices displayed in AXF and FRJ, and a central hub where you can buy almost everything you need to play, breed, decorate, and show off your collection.

El Tianguis has three sub-tabs:

| Tab | Description | Detailed Page |
|-----|-------------|---------------|
| **Official Shop** | Boosters, eggs, consumables, and boards sold directly by the system | This page |
| **Forge (Card Melter)** | Melt duplicate cards to craft foil/shiny cards | [[18-forja-y-fundicion]] |
| **P2P Market** | Peer-to-peer trading (boards, cards, consumables) | [[19-mercado-p2p]] |

This page covers the **Official Shop** exclusively. The other two have their own reference pages.

## 1. Boosters (Sobres) — Card Packs

Each booster contains **7 unique cards** (no duplicates within the same pack). There are 5 booster types, each with its own theme and card pool.

### Booster Types

| Booster | Theme | Card Pool | AXF (Phase 1) | FRJ (Phase 1) |
|---------|-------|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| **Pure (Puro)** | Mixed | All 54 cards | 6 | 60 |
| **Fiesta** | Fiesta themed | 18 themed cards | 10 | 100 |
| **Nido** | Nest themed | 18 themed cards | 10 | 100 |
| **Cosmos** | Cosmos themed | 18 themed cards | 10 | 100 |
| **Foil (Brillante)** | Foil/Shiny | 5 of 7 cards are foil | 80 | 800 |

### Prices by Phase

Booster prices increase with each economic phase:

| Booster | Phase 1 (Genesis) | Phase 2 (Expansion) | Phase 3 (Retail) |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Pure | 6 AXF · 60 FRJ | 9 AXF · 90 FRJ | 12 AXF · 120 FRJ |
| Fiesta | 10 AXF · 100 FRJ | 15 AXF · 150 FRJ | 20 AXF · 200 FRJ |
| Nido | 10 AXF · 100 FRJ | 15 AXF · 150 FRJ | 20 AXF · 200 FRJ |
| Cosmos | 10 AXF · 100 FRJ | 15 AXF · 150 FRJ | 20 AXF · 200 FRJ |
| Foil | 80 AXF · 800 FRJ | 120 AXF · 1,200 FRJ | 160 AXF · 1,600 FRJ |

- **Phase 2**: 1.5× Phase 1 prices.
- **Phase 3**: 2× Phase 1 prices.

### Foil Booster Rules

- **Global cap**: maximum 100 units per month across the entire system. Once depleted, no one can buy until the following month.
- **Guaranteed content**: 5 of the 7 cards in the pack are foil (shiny). The other 2 are standard.

### Shiny Probability by Phase

During **Phase 1 (First Edition)**, each individual card in any booster has a **15% chance** of being shiny. From **Phase 2 onward**, exactly **1 card per pack** is guaranteed shiny (with a 20% chance of a second one, but 1 is the standard).

## 2. Webitos (Egg NFTs)

Webitos are NFT eggs (ERC-721) that hatch into Axolotitos. They are purchased exclusively with AXF (no FRJ).

| Egg Type | AXF Price | FRJ | Total Supply |
|----------|-----------|-----|--------------|
| **Phase 1 (Genesis)** | 400 | — | 420 units |
| **Phase 2 (Expansion)** | 800 | — | 1,260 units |
| **Phase 3 (Retail)** | 1,200 | — | 2,520 units |
| **Astral** | 3,000 | — | 100 per year |

### Astral Webito

The Astral Webito is the most exclusive egg in the game. Its guarantees:

- **Guaranteed stats**: minimum 45 / maximum 85 in every attribute (regular eggs have wider, less predictable ranges).
- **Skin**: 80% chance of "astral" skin, 20% chance of "gold" skin.
- **Guaranteed rare visual trait**: halo or divine mouth (two of the most sought-after visual traits).
- **Supply**: only 100 units per calendar year. Restocks every January.

For more details on breeding and incubation, see [[11-webitos-y-crianza]].

## 3. Consumables

Consumables are single-use items that affect your Axolotitos, eggs, or boards.

| Item | Price | Currency | Effect |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| **Algae Pellet (Common Food)** | 30 | FRJ | +15 energy to Axolotito |
| **Brine Shrimp (Premium Food)** | 150 | FRJ | +60 energy to Axolotito |
| **Anti-Frost Drops** | 200 | FRJ | Prevents egg from freezing during incubation |
| **Infrared Lamp Pro** | 200 | AXF | Instantly un-freezes a frozen egg |
| **Glue Solvent** | 120 | FRJ | Dismantles a board, recovering all 16 cards |

### Consumable Notes

- **Anti-Frost Drops** must be applied before the egg enters a frozen state. They do not reverse freezing that has already occurred — for that, you need the Infrared Lamp.
- **Glue Solvent** is the only way to dismantle a board without losing cards. Dismantling a board without solvent destroys all cards permanently.
- **Algae Pellets** are the most economical way to keep your Axolotito fed. Ideal for daily upkeep.

## 7. Per-User Purchase Limits

- **Boosters**: maximum 100 boosters per user (cumulative across all types). Once reached, you cannot buy any more boosters.
- **Eggs**: limited by your available cave incubation slots. If you have no empty slots, you cannot buy more eggs. See [[11-webitos-y-crianza]].
- **Any item** in the catalog may have a `max_per_user` field imposing an individual cap. These limits can change between phases.
- **Foil Boosters**: in addition to the per-user limit, there is a global cap of 100 units/month. If the global cap is reached, nobody can buy regardless of individual availability.

## 8. Shopping Tips

These tips come from veteran players and are designed to maximize the value of your AXF and FRJ, especially when you are starting out:

1. **Best first purchase: Algae Pellets**. Keeping your Axolotito fed is cheap (30 FRJ) and prevents it from starving. FRJ are earned for free by playing, so you should never run out.

2. **Best value booster: Pure (Puro)**. It's the cheapest (6 AXF + 60 FRJ) and gives access to all 54 cards in the game. Themed boosters are only worth it if you're trying to complete a specific collection.

3. **Save AXF for VIP passes and Webitos**. These are the purchases that give you the most long-term value. A VIP pass pays for itself through discounts and bonuses. A Webito can hatch with exceptional stats worth far more than its purchase price.

4. **Never buy FRJ with AXF unless you are completely dry and need to play right now**. FRJ are earned for free in every match. Spending AXF on FRJ is the least efficient conversion in the game.

5. **Keep a Glue Solvent in your inventory**. You never know when you'll want to reorganize a valuable board. Losing 16 cards because you didn't have one hurts far more than the 120 FRJ it costs.

6. **Brine Shrimp is worth it during events or long play sessions**. It recovers 60 energy in one go instead of adding 15 at a time. It's 5× more FRJ-efficient per energy point than Algae Pellets (2.5 FRJ per energy point vs 2.0 FRJ per point).
