## English

## What Is a Webito?

A **Webito** is an egg — an ERC-721 NFT — that contains the essence of a future Axolotito. It is not just an inventory item: it is life in a latent state, waiting for the right conditions to awaken.

The complete lifecycle:

1. **Buy** an egg from the store or receive it as a reward
2. **Place** it in a Nido — one of the 7 available slots in your Cenote Cave
3. **Incubate** for 7 days, maintaining the necessary heat
4. **Imprint (Apadrinar)** — the most important phase: an adult Axolotito mentors the egg through Lotería matches, shaping its DNA with every win and every loss
5. **Hatch** — the egg breaks open and your new Axolotito is born
6. **Welcome** a new companion with unique stats, nature, and visual traits

Every step of the process leaves an imprint on the Axolotito's DNA. Two eggs of the same type can produce completely different creatures depending on how they were incubated and who mentored them.

### Tutorial Egg

Every new player receives a **free Webito** during the tutorial. This egg is designed to be balanced: solid starting stats without being overpowered, perfect for learning the game mechanics. It is your first companion, and it will always hold a special place in your collection.

## The Incubation Process

You place the egg in a Nido (you have up to 7 slots in your Cenote Cave — see [[16-el-cenote-cueva]]) and the countdown begins: **7 days** of base incubation.

### The Heat System

**Heat** is the incubation progress currency. It is not binary (incubating / not incubating): it is a spectrum.

- **Sano / Healthy** (100% – 50%): The egg is warm and happy. Incubation progresses normally.
- **Frío / Cold** (49% – 25%): The egg starts shivering. Progress slows down.
- **Helándose / Freezing** (24% – 10%): Danger. Progress nearly stops.
- **Congelado / Frozen** (<10%): The egg **freezes**. Incubation is **fully paused** until you restore heat.

If heat reaches **0%**, the egg enters total freeze state. You will not lose the egg, but the 7-day clock stops until you thaw it.

### Climate Affects Heat

The Cenote's day-night cycle directly influences heat retention:

- **Dawn**: moderate heat loss — the sun barely touches the water
- **Morning**: low heat loss — waters are warm
- **Afternoon**: moderate-to-high heat loss — the sun is leaving
- **Night**: high heat loss — deep waters grow cold

Plan your maintenance actions according to the time of day.

### Player Actions

You are not a passive spectator. You have tools to protect your egg:

| Action | Cost | Effect |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Warm Up (Calentar)** | FRJ (variable) | Raises the egg's temperature. FRJ cost depends on how cold it is. |
| **Shield (Escudo)** | 200 FRJ (Anti-Frost Drops) | Temporary freeze protection. The shield absorbs cold damage for a period. |
| **Infrared Lamp** | 200 AXF | **Instant** unfreeze. Pulls the egg out of Frozen state immediately. Emergency use. |

### Incubation Strategy

- Check your eggs at least **twice a day** (morning and night) to keep heat in the Healthy zone
- If you know you will be offline for several hours, **apply a shield** before leaving
- Keep an **Infrared Lamp** in your inventory for emergencies — a frozen egg at 3 AM should not ruin your night
- Eggs in cave slots with insulation upgrades lose heat more slowly

## Imprinting (Apadrinamiento) — The Key Mechanic

This is where the magic truly happens. Imprinting is what turns a generic egg into **your** unique Axolotito.

### Incomplete DNA

When you buy a Webito, its DNA is **incomplete**. The initial stats are mid-range, unrefined. The egg has potential, but it needs to be shaped. Enter the **Padrino** (godparent / mentor).

### What Is a Padrino?

A **Padrino** is an adult Axolotito from your collection who agrees to mentor the egg. The Padrino takes the egg through Lotería matches, and each match — each victory, each defeat, each precise play — leaves a mark on the egg's DNA.

### Padrino Requirements

Not just any Axolotito can become a Padrino. It must meet these conditions:

- **Not frozen** by VIP penalty
- **Not sleeping** (must have available energy)
- **Fewer than 3 previous mentorships** in its lifetime (max 3 per Axolotito)
- **Not currently mentoring** another active Webito

Choose carefully. A Padrino can only mentor **3 times in its entire life**.

### Imprinting Matches

The Padrino and the egg play together. The number of matches depends on the egg's rarity:

| Egg Rarity | Matches Required |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Common** | 3 matches |
| **Rare** | 5 matches |
| **Epic** | 7 matches |
| **Legendary** | 7 matches |

Each match can be played against CPU or other players. During imprinting, **playing against CPU Rookies** is an intentional strategy — you want clean wins to maximize the egg's stats.

### How Matches Shape Stats

After each match, the egg's stats are modified based on performance:

| Match Event | Effect on Egg |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Win** | +8 to +15 Luck |
| **Jackpot** (winning with a special card) | +20 Luck |
| **Loss** | -8 to -12 Luck |
| **Accuracy >= 80%** | +10 to +18 Focus |
| **Accuracy <= 50%** | -8 to -14 Focus |
| **Match 3+** (egg is warmed up) | +10 to +15 Stamina |
| **Match 1** (first match) | -5 Stamina (the egg needs to warm up!) |
| **Padrino SAL < 20** (low salinity) | -6 to -10 Salinity (GOOD — lower is better) |
| **Padrino SAL > 50** (high salinity) | +8 to +12 Salinity (BAD — increases salt) |

### Initial Stats Seeded from Padrino

Before matches begin, the egg receives an initial seed from its Padrino's stats:

- **10% – 15%** of each Padrino stat transfers as base
- **15%** if the Padrino is level 20 or higher
- **10%** if the Padrino is level 1–19

A high-level Padrino with strong stats gives your egg a significant head start.

### DNA Sealing

After completing the required N matches, the egg's DNA is **sealed**. It can no longer be modified. The stats are fixed: they reflect every victory, every mistake, and every moment of brilliance the Padrino and egg shared on the Lotería field.

The egg is ready to hatch.

## Hatching Ceremony

Hatching is not a counter hitting zero. It is a **ceremony** — a 7-step cinematic sequence that celebrates the birth of a new Axolotito.

### The Sequence

1. **Dark screen**. The egg pulses with an internal light that grows and fades, like a heartbeat.
2. **The shell cracks**. A bright silhouette emerges — the Axolotito, still without details, pure light.
3. **The name appears letter by letter**, with a typewriter effect. *Tap... tap... tap...*
4. **Nature reveals itself** with a fanfare. Will it be Bold? Serene? Mischievous? The moment of discovery.
5. **The 4 stats display** as Lotería cards flipping one by one, revealing their final values.
6. **Origin phrase**: *"Learned by playing with [padrino name]. Won [N] out of [M] matches."* — a poetic summary of the journey.
7. **"Meet [Name]" button** — and the Axolotito appears in your collection, complete and unique.

### Duration by Rarity

| Rarity | Duration | Special Effects |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| **Common** | ~12 seconds | Clean, emotional standard sequence |
| **Rare** | ~18 seconds | Floating water particles during stat reveal |
| **Legendary** | ~25 seconds | **Full screen**. Golden confetti. Intensified fanfare. Stat cards have a golden glow. |

Every hatching is a moment to remember. The first time you watch a Legendary Axolotito being born... there is nothing like it.

## Padrino Rewards

Being a Padrino is not just an honor — it comes with tangible rewards:

- **+150 XP** for the Padrino (a significant experience boost)
- **+2.0 permanent stat points** in the stat that had the highest positive delta during imprinting (the stat that improved the most in the egg)
- Padrino **mentorship_count** increases by 1 (max 3 per lifetime)
- The Padrino is celebrated with a temporary visual badge and a special animation

Being a Padrino leaves a permanent mark. An Axolotito that has mentored 3 eggs is a living legend.

## Post-Hatching: How Stats Improve

Once the Axolotito is born, its stats are not static. They can improve through several avenues:

| Method | Type | Effect |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Equipment** (accessories) | Permanent while equipped | Bonuses to specific stats based on the item |
| **Special items** (Luck Powder, etc.) | Temporary | Boost for a limited number of matches or time |
| **Leveling up** (XP) | Permanent | Base stat improvement upon reaching new levels |

### Genetic Purity

**Genetic Purity** measures the overall quality of an Axolotito's stats. It is calculated as a percentage relative to the theoretical maximum for its rarity.

If genetic purity reaches **>= 110%** (Mythic range), there is a **25%** chance the Axolotito manifests a **rare visual trait**:

- **Phoenix Gills** — gills that glow with fire tones and visually regenerate
- **Plasma Tail** — tail with a pulsating energy effect
- **Halo Forehead** — a ring of light floating above the head
- **Divine Mouth** — mouth pattern with a celestial glow
- **Coral Limbs** — legs and arms with living coral texture and colors

These traits are purely visual (they do not affect gameplay) but are extremely rare and highly sought after by collectors.

## Tips for Breeding

### Choose Your Padrino Wisely

- **Focus and Luck** are the most important stats to pass on. A Padrino with high Focus will help the egg achieve better accuracy in its imprinting matches, creating a positive feedback loop.
- **Low Padrino Salinity** is crucial — remember that high Padrino SAL **increases** the egg's salinity, and low SAL is better for match performance.

### Play Smart During Imprinting

- **Play against CPU Rookies** during imprinting matches. They are easier to beat, and every win adds Luck to the egg.
- **Do not play with a tired Padrino**. A Padrino with low energy performs worse. Check its energy before starting an imprinting session.
- **The first match** always penalizes the egg's Stamina (-5). Do not be alarmed — this is normal. The egg needs to "warm up." From match 3 onward, Stamina recovers and improves.

### Plan for the Long Term

- **The Astral Webito is worth saving for**. 3,000 AXF is not cheap, but the stat floor of 45, the guaranteed rare visual trait, and the premium skins make it the best long-term investment.
- **Padrinos can only mentor 3 times**. Do not waste a mentorship on a common egg if you have an exceptional Padrino. Save its slots for Epic or Astral eggs.
- **Genesis eggs** (Phase 1, 400 AXF) are limited to 420 units. Once depleted, there will be no more. If you can get one, do it.

### Incubation Maintenance

- **Two check-ins per day**: one in the morning (when heat loss is low) and one at night (before the coldest period).
- **Shield before sleeping**: if you will be unable to check the egg for 8+ hours, spend the 200 FRJ on Anti-Frost Drops. It is cheaper than an emergency Infrared Lamp.
- **Coordinate with climate**: if possible, perform Warm Up actions during Morning (lower FRJ cost due to favorable climate).
