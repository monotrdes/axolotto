## English

# Gashapon Capsules — La Suertuda (The Lucky One)

**La Suertuda** is Axolotto's gacha system: a capsule vending machine where you spend FRJ to spin and receive random prizes. Every spin is a small dose of adrenaline — the mechanism sound, the capsule dropping, the reveal animation... and if you hit a legendary, it's a full-screen celebration!

Understanding how it works is key: the probabilities are transparent, the "karma" pity system is on your side, and there are options for every budget. But remember: **never spend your last FRJ on capsules** — always keep some for entry fees and food for your Axolotitos.

---

## What is La Suertuda?

La Suertuda is the capsule machine of the Axolotto universe. Think of it like Japanese gashapon machines: you insert your tokens, turn the crank, and receive a capsule with a surprise inside. You never know exactly what you'll get, and that uncertainty is part of the fun.

You can win:
- **FRJ** — a partial refund of your wager (sometimes more)
- **Cards** — from Common to Legendary, including Rare Cards
- **Accessories** — to decorate your Axolotitos
- **Sobrecitos (Boosters)** — content packs, sometimes in Foil version
- **Forged Boards (Tablas Forjadas)** — Gold-exclusive: retired boards from level 10+ NPCs with battle history

---

## The Three Tiers

Each tier has its own cost, karma limit, and legendary drop probability:

| Tier | Cost (FRJ) | Karma Limit | Legendary Pre-Check |
|------|-----------|-------------|---------------------|
| Bronze | 1,500 | 12 rolls | 0.2% |
| Silver | 5,000 | 6 rolls | 0.5% |
| Gold | 20,000 | 4 rolls | 1.0% |

### Bronze — The Entry-Level Tier

The most accessible tier. Ideal for new or F2P players who want to test their luck without risking too much. Rewards are modest, but the 12-roll karma limit means you will eventually get a Rare Card no matter what. At only 1,500 FRJ per roll, you can afford several attempts.

### Silver — The Sweet Spot

The best value per FRJ spent. At 5,000 FRJ per roll, probabilities improve significantly: more Rare and Epic cards, better accessories, and the karma limit drops to just 6 rolls. If you've been playing for a while and have a comfortable FRJ cushion, Silver is your tier.

### Gold — For Collectors and Whales

20,000 FRJ per roll. Expensive, yes, but the rewards back it up: 1% legendary pre-check, 15% Rare Card chance, Epic/Legendary accessories, and a 3% chance to receive a **Forged Board**. Only for players with plenty of FRJ who are hunting the very best.

---

## Triple Suerte (Triple Luck) — The Combo

**Cost: 22,500 FRJ** — 15% discount vs buying all three tiers separately (26,500 FRJ).

Triple Suerte rolls all three tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold) in a single operation. You receive three independent results, one from each tier. But it has one CRUCIAL advantage:

> **Duplicate Protection**: if the same rare item would drop twice in a single Triple Suerte, the system automatically re-rolls the duplicate. You cannot receive two identical Rare Cards (same ID) in one combo.

This is the single best value item in the game. If you can save up 22,500 FRJ, do it — it's worth it.

## Capsule Prize Pool Distribution

This is EXACTLY what can come out of each capsule, with the real probabilities:

| Outcome | Bronze | Silver | Gold |
|---------|--------|--------|------|
| **FRJ** | 55% (40-120 FRJ) | 30% (150-450 FRJ) | 12% (400-2,000 FRJ) |
| **Card** | 28% (Common/Rare) | 40% (Rare/Epic) | 43% (Epic/Legendary) |
| **Accessory** | 10% (Common) | 15% (Rare) | 18% (Epic/Legendary) |
| **Booster (Sobre)** | 5% | 10% | 12% |
| **Rare Card** | 2% (Rare/Epic) | 5% (Epic/Legendary) | 15% (Epic/Legendary) |

### Pool Notes:
- **FRJ** is the most common result — in Bronze, over half of all spins return FRJ. This is by design: you get something back, but generally less than you spent. Gold spins can return up to 2,000 FRJ, which is a 10% rebate.
- **Card** vs **Rare Card**: these are separate categories with distinct pools. Rare Card has its own probability slot and is what triggers the karma pity system.
- **Accessories** improve in rarity with tier: only Common in Bronze, up to Legendary in Gold.
- **Booster** can be normal or Foil (see below).

## Karma System (Pity)

Karma is the system that guarantees **you never walk away empty-handed after many spins**. Here's how it works:

1. Each tier has its own **karma counter**, independent and tracked per player.
2. The counter increases by +1 with every spin where you do NOT receive a **Rare Card**.
3. When the counter reaches that tier's limit, the next spin is **GUARANTEED** to drop a Rare Card.
4. The counter **resets to zero** when:
   - You receive a Rare Card (by normal probability or by karma)
   - You receive a Legendary drop of any type (card, accessory, or legendary foil booster)

### Limits by tier:
| Tier | Karma Limit | Max Spins Without Rare Card |
|------|-------------|----------------------------|
| Bronze | 12 | 11 — the 12th is guaranteed |
| Silver | 6 | 5 — the 6th is guaranteed |
| Gold | 4 | 3 — the 4th is guaranteed |

### The Karma Bar UI

Next to the capsule machine, you'll see a bar that fills up over time:
- **Empty (cool blue)**: just starting out, everything normal.
- **Halfway (yellow)**: building up, karma is accumulating.
- **Hot (orange/red)**: you're close to the limit! The next spin could be THE spin.
- **GUARANTEED (pulsing gold)**: you've hit the limit. Your next spin in this tier **WILL** give you a Rare Card. The bar displays "KARMA ACTIVATED!" with visual effects.

The bar adds a layer of strategy: do you spin one more time in Bronze to trigger karma, or save for a Triple Suerte? These decisions are part of the game.

## Foil Booster Chance

When your spin result is "Sobre" (Booster), there's a secondary probability of it being **Foil** (shiny, special edition) instead of a normal booster:

| Tier | Foil Chance |
|------|-------------|
| Bronze | 5% |
| Silver | 12% |
| Gold | 22% |

Foil boosters contain higher-rarity items than normal boosters of the same type. Visually, the capsule shimmers with a rainbow effect before opening — you won't miss it.

---

## Forged Board (Tabla Forjada) — Gold Exclusive

**Probability: 3%** in Gold capsules.

**Forged Boards** are lottery boards retired from level 10+ NPCs that have real battle history. They are unique pieces with a story: each one comes with its match record, wins, losses, and an origin text that tells where it came from.

Characteristics:
- Boards at level 10 or higher
- Documented battle history (games played, won, lost)
- Procedurally generated origin story
- Only available if you have **free board slots** in your inventory. If you have no space, the Forged Board cannot be claimed — make sure you have slots before spinning Gold!

Getting a Forged Board is one of the most exciting moments in La Suertuda. The reveal animation is epic: the Gold capsule cracks apart, the board emerges with a blinding glow, and its origin story scrolls across the screen.

## Which Tier Should You Buy?

### New Player / F2P
**Go Bronze.** Low risk, 1,500 FRJ per roll, and karma has your back. The reveal experience is just as fun at any tier. When you can, save up for Triple Suerte — 22,500 FRJ stings, but the 15% discount and duplicate protection make it worthwhile.

### Mid-Game (you have several Axolotitos and stable FRJ income)
**Silver is your best friend.** Best value per FRJ spent. Good Rare Card odds (5%), Rare accessories, and only 6 rolls for karma. At 5,000 FRJ per spin, you can make several pulls without going broke.

### Whale / Collector
**Gold, no question.** Highest legendary chance (1% pre-check), 15% Rare Card rate, Epic/Legendary accessories, and the chance at a Forged Board (3%). Every spin is a premium experience.

### Best Absolute Value
**Triple Suerte (22,500 FRJ).** 15% cheaper than buying all three tiers separately, duplicate protection, and three back-to-back reveals that are pure dopamine. If you can save that amount, it's the best investment in La Suertuda.

### Golden Rule
> **NEVER spend your last FRJ on capsules.** You also need FRJ for match entry fees, Axolotito food, and other game mechanics. La Suertuda is fun and excitement, not necessity. Play responsibly.

## Free Capsules

Not everything in La Suertuda costs FRJ. There are ways to earn free spins:

### VIP Membership
VIP Club members receive monthly capsules based on their tier:
- **Coral**: 2× Bronze per month
- **Dorado**: 2× Bronze + 1× Silver per month
- **Axolite**: 3× Bronze + 2× Silver + 1× Gold per month

### Lunar Cycle — Day 7
Every seventh day of the Lunar Cycle grants rewards that can include capsules. See [[21-ciclo-lunar-y-recompensas]] for full details.

Free capsules **do not count toward karma** — the karma counter only advances with paid spins. But the prizes you earn from free capsules are yours to keep forever.

## Gacha Strategy and Psychology

La Suertuda is designed to be fun, fair, and transparent. Here are some tips from veteran players:

1. **Karma is your safety net.** It's not a punishment for bad luck — it's a guarantee that you'll eventually get something good. Bronze with 12 karma rolls means, in the worst case, you spend 18,000 FRJ for a guaranteed Rare Card.
2. **Don't chase the legendary.** Legendary pre-check odds are low (0.2%-1.0%). If you hit one, celebrate it as the special event it is. If not, enjoy the journey.
3. **Triple Suerte is mathematically superior.** 15% discount + duplicate protection. If you have the patience to save, do it.
4. **Diversify.** Alternate between La Suertuda, the Store, El Tianguis (P2P market), and regular matches. Don't put all your FRJ into one system.
5. **VIP capsules are passive income.** If you're considering VIP, factor the free capsules into the total value.
