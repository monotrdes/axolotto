# How to Play Lotería in Axolotto

Welcome to the heart of Axolotto! This is your complete guide to every mechanic, every rule, and every trick in the game. If you read only one page on this wiki, make it this one.

---

## What is Lotería?

Lotería is a traditional Mexican card game with over 200 years of history, similar to bingo but infinitely more colorful. Instead of numbers, you have 54 unique cards featuring iconic characters:

> 🐓 **El Gallo (The Rooster)** · 🌙 **La Luna (The Moon)** · ☀️ **El Sol (The Sun)** · 👩 **La Dama (The Lady)** · 🌵 **El Nopal (The Cactus)** · 💀 **La Calavera (The Skull)** · 🌹 **La Rosa (The Rose)** · 🐟 **El Pescado (The Fish)** · 🕷️ **La Araña (The Spider)** · 🎻 **El Violoncello (The Cello)** ... and 44 more.

A **cantador** (caller — in Axolotto we call them the **Gritón** 🤖) draws and announces cards one by one. You mark the ones that match your **Tabla** (your personal 4×4 board). The first to complete a winning pattern... wins!

---

## Your Tabla: The 4×4 Board

Your Tabla is a **4×4 = 16 card** grid you select from your personal collection. Think of it as your bingo card, but with characters instead of numbers.

### Key Tabla Facts:

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Size** | 4 rows × 4 columns = 16 positions |
| **Card source** | Your personal collection (obtained from Sobrecitos, P2P market, or Codes) |
| **Do positions matter?** | Yes! Each position is strategic — certain winning patterns require specific positions |
| **Random creation** | Costs **25 FRJ** — the system picks 16 random cards from your collection |
| **Manual creation** | Costs **50 FRJ** — you decide exactly which card goes in each cell |
| **How many can I own?** | As many as you want. Collect multiple Tablas and pick which to use each match |

> 🎯 **Strategy tip:** Designing your Tabla manually is worth it. You can optimize positions for specific patterns or set up "hot zones" where you concentrate your best cards.

## The Game Loop, Step by Step

Every Lotería match in Axolotto follows this exact flow:

### 🦎 Step 1 — Pick Your Axolotito
Your Axolotito is your avatar and your "hand" in the game. Its stats matter:
- **OJO (Focus)** → affects your chance to mark cards correctly
- **SUERTE (Luck)** → affects prizes and miraculous saves
- **SAL (Salinity)** → affects where your cards land in the deck

### 📋 Step 2 — Pick Your Tabla(s)
- **CPU Mode (Solo):** 1 Tabla per match
- **Multiplayer Mode:** up to 3 Tablas simultaneously

### 🏠 Step 3 — Choose the Room
| Room | Entry (buy-in) | Difficulty / Players |
|------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Rookies (Novatos)** | 25 FRJ | 1 easy bot (CPU) · up to 30 humans (multi) |
| **Champions (Campeones)** | 100 FRJ | 5 hard bots (CPU) · up to 30 humans (multi) |

### ✖️ Step 4 — Set the Multiplier (optional)
Multiply your bet for proportionally larger prizes:
- **1×** (normal) · **2×** · **5×** · **10×**

The multiplier applies to both your entry fee and your prizes. High risk, high reward.

### 🎤 Step 5 — The Gritón Calls the Cards
The Gritón shuffles a full 54-card deck and begins announcing cards one by one. Your Axolotito automatically tries to mark the cards that match your Tabla.

### 🎯 Step 6 — Marking Mechanic (Miss Chance)
Here is the twist: **you do not always mark a card even when it is on your Tabla.**

- Your **OJO (Focus)** stat determines your accuracy
- There is a miss chance of **0% to 30%** depending on how high your OJO is
- More OJO = fewer misses. Simple.

### 🧂 Step 7 — Deck Bias (Salinity)
Your **SAL (Salinity)** stat is BAD for you:
- It pushes your cards toward the **end of the deck**
- The more SAL you have, the later your cards appear
- This means players with lower SAL have an advantage — their cards come out sooner

### 🏆 Step 8 — Determining the Winner
The first to complete a **winning pattern** wins the round. Two prizes are up for grabs:

### 🏆 Step 9 — Prize Distribution
| Prize | Condition | Percentage of Pot |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Premio 1** | First pattern completed (line, cuadrito, corners, etc.) | **35%** of pot |
| **Premio 2 (¡Lotería!)** | Full board — all 16 cards marked | **55%** of pot |
| **Jackpot** | Automatic contribution | **5%** of pot |
| **House/Treasury** | System fee | **5%** of pot |

- Prizes are shared among all winners at the same level
- **Double Winner Rule:** the same player CAN win both Premio 1 AND Premio 2 in the same match!

## Multiplayer Mode

The true competitive heart of Axolotto.

### Key Features:
- **Up to 30 human players** per room
- **Real-time:** everyone plays simultaneously against the same Gritón
- **Auto-AFK:** Need to step away? Your Axolotito keeps playing for you automatically
- **Smart budget:** set stop-loss (max loss) and take-profit (target gain) limits
- **Auto-reinscription:** your Axolotito automatically re-enters new rounds until limits are hit or energy runs out
- **Settlement:** when you finish your session, claim your accumulated balance + earned loyalty points

> 🤖 **The Gritón is shared.** All players in the room hear the same cards in the same order. It is fair for everyone.

## Key Mechanics that Affect Your Chances

### 🎯 Miss Chance
- **Depends on:** Your Axolotito's OJO (Focus)
- **Effect:** You may NOT mark a card even when it is on your Tabla
- **Range:** 0% (max OJO) to 30% (min OJO)
- **Strategy:** Train your Axolotito's OJO. It is the single most important stat for winning.

### 🧂 Deck Bias (Salinity)
- **Depends on:** Your Axolotito's SAL (Salinity)
- **Effect:** Your cards get pushed toward the end of the 54-card deck
- **Strategy:** Keep SAL low. Use items or mechanics that reduce salinity.

### 🍀 Lucky Save
- **Depends on:** Your Axolotito's SUERTE (Luck)
- **Effect:** Up to **5% chance** to prevent a loss when another player is about to win
- **Strategy:** Luck is not your Plan A, but it can save your day.

### 🔥 Win Streak
- **Effect:** Consecutive wins give **+15% bonus per win**, stacking up to **+50%**
- **Strategy:** If you are on a hot streak, raise your multiplier. The risk is worth it with the stacked bonus.

## The Jackpot (Global Accumulated Prize Pool) 💰

The Jackpot is a global prize pool that grows with every multiplayer match.

### How It Works:
- Every multiplayer match contributes **5% of the pot** to the Jackpot
- The Jackpot is **global** — shared across all rooms and matches

### How to Win It:
- Complete **Premio 1** (first pattern) within the first **4 to 6 cards** called by the Gritón
- There must be at least **5 human Tablas** from **2+ different wallets** in the room
- The winner takes **90% of the accumulated Jackpot**

### Jackpot Facts:
- **Seed amount:** 1,000 FRJ
- **Contribution per match:** 5% of the total pot
- **Prize:** 90% of the accumulated pool
- **Minimum players:** 5 human Tablas, 2+ different wallets

> 🌟 Breaking the Jackpot is the most epic achievement in Axolotto. It requires a perfect Tabla, sky-high OJO, and a little bit of magic.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Can I switch Tablas mid-match?**
No. Your Tabla is locked at the start of each round.

**What if two players complete the same pattern on the same card?**
The prize is split evenly between them.

**Can I play without an Axolotito?**
No. You need an Axolotito to play — it is the one marking cards for you.

**Does the Gritón favor anyone?**
No. The deck is shuffled with cryptographic randomness (`SystemRandom`). It is completely fair.

**Can I win both Premio 1 and Premio 2 in the same match?**
Yes! The Double Winner Rule allows it. If you complete a pattern first AND then finish your full board, you take both prizes.

**Does the Jackpot apply in CPU mode?**
No. Only multiplayer matches with 5+ human Tablas and 2+ different wallets.

**What happens if I leave mid-match in multiplayer?**
Your Axolotito keeps playing in Auto-AFK until the round ends. You receive your prizes normally.
